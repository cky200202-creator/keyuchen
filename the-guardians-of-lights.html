《和光同尘》

The Guardians of Lights

预告片  Trailer

如无法播放，前往 YouTube 观看 ↗

成片  Full Film

如无法播放，前往 YouTube 观看 ↗

这是一部关于守塔人这份特殊职业的纪录片，同时也讲述了亲情，死亡，和不得不面对的孤独。故事聚焦在两位主人公身上，他们做着相同的职业却拥有着截然不同的命运。影片用诗意的镜头缓缓讲述了这份即将消亡的职业，和这群依旧坚守在祖国最边陲人的故事。

This is a documentary about the special profession of lighthouse keepers, and it also tells stories of family, death, and the loneliness that must be faced. The story focuses on two main characters who share the same profession but have completely different fates. The film uses poetic shots to slowly tell the story of this disappearing profession and the people who still stay steadfastly on the country's remotest borders.
